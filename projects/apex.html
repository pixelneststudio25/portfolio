A private investment tracker that even its own database can't read.

Apex keeps one complete record of what you own: land, rent, loans and shared deals. It works out your real returns, and encrypts everything on your device before it ever syncs.

The problem

Most people who invest in land, rental property or loans keep their records in notebooks, WhatsApp chats and loose files. Returns end up guessed instead of measured, and the papers that prove ownership sit on a phone anyone could open. Apex puts every asset on one record: what went in, what it pays, what it's worth now, and the documents behind it, all private by design.

How it works

Every entry, whether money in, rent received or a new valuation, is encrypted on the device before it leaves it. Apex saves it locally first, then syncs only the scrambled version, so it keeps working with no connection. A built-in calculation engine turns those records into returns, projections and a printable statement, and the whole app installs on a phone like a native one.

The hardest decision

The safest way to protect financial records is a design where the server never holds the key. The catch is that if a user loses their password and their backup, nobody can get the data back, including me. Most apps avoid that by keeping a copy of the key somewhere. For an app holding land papers and income records, I decided that was the wrong compromise.

The tradeoff

Keeping the key off the server meant building a recovery path myself: a printable recovery key at sign-up, a PIN that unlocks a trusted device, and a lock that wipes the PIN after five wrong tries. It cost extra screens, extra testing and a longer sign-up. In exchange, the records are unreadable to anyone but their owner, even to whoever runs the database.

A look inside

A dashboard that leads with the one number that matters, a PIN lock that opens in a second, projections for a single asset or the whole portfolio, and an encrypted vault for the papers behind each investment.

Where it stands

Apex is live and installable on phone and desktop. It works offline, supports custom asset categories, exports a printable statement, and is covered by 250+ automated tests across the calculation engine, sync, documents and the offline worker. Next up is passkey sign-in and a wider range of asset types.
