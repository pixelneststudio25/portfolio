An ecommerce store that sells past geography.

AI-OKO sells fabrics, shoes and bags to customers who never had to be anywhere near the shop, with a checkout that hands off to WhatsApp so every order becomes a real conversation, not just a transaction.

Role

Solo developer

Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Decap CMS

Status

Live

Catalog

50+ products listed

The problem

A fabrics, shoes and bags business is limited by geography the moment it depends on foot traffic: customers can only buy what they can physically walk in and see. AI-OKO exists to remove that ceiling, so the same catalog that used to sell to one neighbourhood can sell to anyone who can open a browser.

How it works

Customers browse a full storefront of over 50 products, add items to a cart, and check out; but instead of ending at a payment form, checkout hands off to WhatsApp. The order details carry over automatically, and payment and delivery get worked out directly with the business. On the other side, the catalog itself is managed through a CMS admin panel, so new products, prices, and stock go live without anyone touching code.

The hardest decision

A standard payment gateway integration was the obvious default, but it wasn't the right fit here. This business isn't just trying to process transactions, it's trying to build relationships and grow a real reputation with its customers, and a fabrics/shoes/bags purchase often comes with questions about fit, fabric, or delivery that a checkout form can't answer.

The tradeoff

Routing checkout through WhatsApp instead of a payment gateway trades away one-click automated payment for something the business needed more: low complexity to build and maintain, and a direct conversation with every customer at the exact moment they're ready to buy. It's a small deliberate step back from "fully automated" in exchange for the kind of precise, personal delivery conversation that actually earns repeat customers.

Where it stands

AI-OKO is live and finished, running a catalog of over 50 products with the business managing its own inventory day to day through the CMS panel, no developer involvement required to keep it running.
