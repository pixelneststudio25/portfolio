I build tools that hand people back their time, and the ability to build things themselves.

I started teaching myself to code at 16, right after high school, and I've been building ever since. No bootcamp, no CS degree, just a lot of late nights figuring out how things work by taking them apart.

What I keep coming back to is building tools that let other people build. Zuri turns a business idea into a live website. The store builder I'm working on now does the same for entrepreneurs who want to sell online. Even the internal tools I've built, attendance systems and registration portals among them, exist to hand ordinary people back their time.

Right now I'm focused on getting both the website builder and the store builder from working products to ones real businesses depend on.
